## 5. Every outcome and line-ending condition

Evaluated in this order; disjoint and exhaustive.

| outcome | condition |
| --- | --- |
| **INVALID** | a tick-0, prefix, end-record or chain mismatch; a consumed-tick violation; an inexact replay of a pool candidate, of the record at the open or of an identity cell; a replayed clear whose `completion_input_tick` differs from its registered clear tick; a pin drift; a provenance or static-guard violation, or any write under another `runs/` tree; any read of fixtures, recordings or the TAS; a change of an earlier tree (R1) at the open or the close; a prefix-chain failure at any level; an open overlay or a ledger rebuild that does not reproduce; an inherited record fact that differs from the pinned one; a record without its stamp at the close |
| **INCOMPLETE** | exploration below 2,000,000 native ticks; a memory, process-count, session-cap or worker-error stop; more than 3 lifecycle failures; the open phase (P1, the identity replays, the record re-verified) not completed; a pool candidate with a registered clear tick at or below 2,291 left unverified at a cap before any exact clear replay; the close identity replays not completed |
| **PROGRESS** | an exact replay with `completion_input_tick <= 2,291`; the fastest one is the result and the archive's new record reference |
| **RECORD_BELOW_MARGIN** | no PROGRESS, and an exact replay with `2,291 < completion_input_tick < 2,315`: the new reference, not progress |
| **NO_SHORTER_CLEAR** | otherwise (no exact clear replay, or none below 2,315) |

The line record (`rule.json.line`, with every outcome): PROGRESS or RECORD_BELOW_MARGIN -> `SD2_PROPOSABLE_NEW_REFERENCE` (an sd2 under the same design with the new reference may be
proposed, its own authorisation); NO_SHORTER_CLEAR -> `SD2_PROPOSABLE_WITH_REASON` (only with a stated reason read from the diagnostics); INCOMPLETE after exploration started ->
`COUNTED_INCOMPLETE` (it counts as sd1; a repeat is sd2 with its reason stated; a verified clear found before the stop is reported, and whether it becomes the reference is decided with the
sd2 proposal); INCOMPLETE before exploration started -> `NOT_STARTED` (does not count; repair and a new approval, then sd1 again); INVALID -> `REPAIR_NEEDED` (repair and a new approval;
whether it counts is the user's decision, recorded as `None`, never guessed). No third session without a review. The rd line is copied through unchanged: `rd5_permitted` stays rd4's
recorded `true`; `consumed_by_sd1` and `granted_by_sd1` are false. Stops: S5 (BOUND_VIOLATION: the bound is withdrawn under a new contract) and S7 (INVALID or INCOMPLETE: repair and a
new approval, never an extension or an automatic retry) are carried; S3 (median L of the top level's eligible cells above 3,000) is reported only. Flags: `BOUND_VIOLATION`,
`INHERITED_REPLAY_INEXACT`. The dilution verdict is reported only; there is no mechanism check.
