## 10. What this does and does not say

- It says the continuation harness works end to end on the real game (resume, overlay, v2 selection, exact returns, verification, close audits, backup) with every integrity check clean, and **that an archive search seeded only by agent-generated trajectories reached the wall top**
  from tick 0 within 4,037 further returns; the route was found at the 3,684th of them, after 4.55 M further exploration ticks.
- It does **not** say v2 caused it. There is no control arm, rd1's archive was the starting point, and the selection's dilution measures did not meet the registered limits (verdict DILUTION_PERSISTS). What changed measurably is the supply of precursor states
  (launch-capable cells 19x, closest approach 1,017 to 162 units, dispatches from A2 states 18.8 % to 41.9 %), which is the quantity the proposal's S6 asked about.
- It is not a policy result: a verified route does not show that a policy can learn it (consolidation is a separate, demonstrated bottleneck; M7h gate 4, M7m, M7p).
- Left of the wall nothing happened: 0 dispatches from x < -2,100, no left target, no crossing, no eighth target. The wall-top landing is a landing, not a crossing; the route continues from line 0 only if a later session explores from there.
